Isolated neutron star thermal catalogue

Literature measurements, their cooling interpretation, and the discrepancies that affect it.

How to read the review statuses

Literature consistent means comparable entries agree within their quoted ranges in this cross-match. They may share the same data or analysis. Published revision identifies a later literature update. Open discrepancy marks competing estimates, a timing inconsistency, or a material difference between age proxies. Disputed denotes a contested identity or age association. Limited evidence covers cases with a single analysis, upper limits, patch emission, missing independent age, or incomplete cross-checks.

Statuses assess the evidence for cooling comparisons and are separate from the physical class. Spin-down age τc = P/(2Ṗ) is listed alongside ages inferred from remnants, proper motion, or historical records. For the spectral fits, BB means blackbody and NSA, NSMAXG and NSX denote atmosphere models. Luminosity and temperature are quoted at infinity where applicable. Bounds retain the conventions of each reference.
